# Principles

- **Nudge, don't override.** The plugin helps models work at their best by guiding them toward a timely handover. It avoids strict enforcement that makes decisions on the model's behalf. Hard enforcement exists only as an opt-in backstop.
- **The session's model writes the handover.** The model running the session has the best understanding of it and is the ideal author of its handover. Do not substitute a separate summarizer or a mechanical extract.
- **Automate the continuation.** Handovers should flow into continued work without human intervention. Prefer unattended mechanisms over interactive review or confirmation; stops exist only as safety backstops.
- **Defer to parent agents.** Where a parent agent exists, rely on its knowledge and judgment to continue, redirect, or escalate work rather than automating around it.
